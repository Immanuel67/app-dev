# 🧟 The Walking Dead

## Series Overview
**Genre:** Horror, Drama, Thriller  
**Created by:** Frank Darabont  
**Based on:** *The Walking Dead* comic series by Robert Kirkman  

The Walking Dead follows a group of survivors navigating a world overrun by zombies — or as they call them, “walkers.”  
It explores how far people will go to stay alive and what humanity means in a collapsing world.

---

## 👥 Main Characters
- **Rick Grimes** – Former sheriff’s deputy and group leader  
- **Daryl Dixon** – Expert tracker and loyal survivor  
- **Michonne** – Katana-wielding warrior  
- **Negan** – Charismatic but brutal former antagonist turned ally  
- **Carol Peletier** – Resourceful and resilient survivor  

---

## 🌟 Why I Love It
The Walking Dead isn't just about zombies — it's about **human emotion, morality, and survival**.  
Every season shows how people adapt, break, and rebuild hope in a hopeless world.

> *“We don’t kill the living.” – Rick Grimes*

---

## 🧰 Favorite Aspects
1. Strong character development  
2. Intense survival storytelling  
3. Deep moral and ethical conflicts  
4. Impressive makeup and visual effects
